# Future Roadmap

> **Project:** SyncBoard
> **Document:** Future Roadmap
> **Phase:** 10 - Development
> **Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Vision

SyncBoard aims to become a modern, scalable, AI-powered collaborative whiteboard platform for individuals, teams, educational institutions, and enterprises.

The long-term goals are:

- Real-time collaboration
- High performance
- AI-assisted productivity
- Enterprise readiness
- Extensible architecture
- Global accessibility

---

# 2. Product Principles

Every future feature should align with these principles:

- User-first experience
- Performance by default
- Security by design
- Scalability
- Accessibility
- Developer-friendly architecture
- Modular implementation

---
